C-Eval is a multi-level, multi-discipline Chinese evaluation suite for foundation models. It covers 52 disciplines spanning humanities, social sciences, STEM, and other fields, with questions at four levels of difficulty to comprehensively assess the capabilities of foundation models in Chinese.
