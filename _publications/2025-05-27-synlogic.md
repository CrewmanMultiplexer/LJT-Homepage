This paper introduces SynLogic, a comprehensive logical reasoning data synthesis framework that generates diverse, verifiable reasoning data at scale. The framework covers 35 diverse logical reasoning tasks, enables controllable difficulty synthesis, provides verifiable rewards through rule-based verification for reinforcement learning, and demonstrates strong cross-domain transfer to mathematical and coding domains.
